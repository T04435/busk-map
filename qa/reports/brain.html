Field strip (F13, F14): /brain

38 of 38 fields fail at 390 and 1440, day and night (152 checks): every node is a button and shows two ring layers when focused (cause: the global :focus-visible double shadow, Site-wide S09). DEV reports this fixed in e575a69, after 7b1521e.

Impeccable critique

  1. P2 · The invitation got quieter. The idle panel's two concrete “Try …” prompts became one run-on sentence (BR18, B-052); the page's one interaction is easier to miss.
  2. P2 · The trace speaks in two colours. The picked node and its wires are orange, but the panel that explains them titles the pick in purple and writes “From …/To …” where the board draws arrows (BR26, BR27, BR29, BR30). The eye can't tie panel to path at a glance.
  3. P2 · Day theme breaks the night page. The footer and bottom nav go paper while the page stays night (BR40), so the page ends on a bright band.
  4. P3 · Sub-lines don't tell calls apart. Five Qloo calls end “urn:entity:artist” (BR11), so the column reads as repeats where the board shows what makes each call different.
  5. P3 · Small texture and copy drift. No hex lattice in the ground (BR02), “plan” for “busk”, and the back link in paper rather than orange (BR05, BR20, BR23).
