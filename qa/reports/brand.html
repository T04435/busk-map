Field strip (F14): /brand

All 46 field states fail (23 fields × day/night × 390/1440): each focused field, link or summary shows a paper gap plus a dark ring (two layers). Cause: the global :focus-visible rule (Site-wide S09).

Impeccable critique

  1. P1 · The page shows the logo the user removed. Header, hero and footer use the lockup, the brand book still says the ring “is the logo”, and the lockup and mark lead the Logos group unlabelled (BD01, BD07, BD09, BD44-BD46; F12).
  2. P2 · Half the design system is not on its own showcase. 5 of 8 component groups, the plan states block, Iconography, Screens and Handoff are missing (BD19, BD22-BD37, BD42, BD43, BD52); the hero and nav were reworded to fit.
  3. P2 · The assets contradict the calls. Every social and kit image has the lockup, pink rectangles, Qloo as text, Melbourne in the OG caption, and placeholder screenshots (BD49, BD51; F16-F22, BRAND).
  4. P2 · The page says something false about motion. “Everything drops to 0 ms” while controls keep 150-320 ms transitions under reduced motion (BD18, BD58).
  5. P3 · Under 1024 px everything is folded shut, 21 closed rows, so a tablet reader sees no colour, type or component at all until they open each one (BD56).
  6. P3 · “Open file” for a download with an external-link icon misstates what happens (BD50).
