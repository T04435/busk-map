Impeccable critique (design review)

  1. P1 · The time is asked twice. Time-of-day chips (B13) and From/To selects (B12) set the same thing; the user has to reconcile them. F27 settles where it goes.
  2. P1 · The search fields look broken. A box inside a box (B06, B07) and a doubled focus ring read as a rendering bug, on the page's most-used inputs (F13, F14).
  3. P2 · Results don't get out of the way. After a pick the song results stay open (B-036), pushing the rest of the form down; the act loses its place.
  4. P2 · The set has no shape. Picks show no count or total time (B-037), though set length is the point of the page's sister feature on the plan.
  5. P2 · Labels that disappear. The Spotify link's label lives in the placeholder (B-040) and the textarea's instructions too: both vanish on typing. F25 and F26 remove most of it.
  6. P3 · Long intro line at 1440 (B02) and the “Melbourne” eyebrow (F20).
