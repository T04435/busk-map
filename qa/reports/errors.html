Impeccable critique

  1. P2 · One card for four different moments. The build pushes the 404, route error, global error and plan not found through one left-aligned StatusCard with a 56 px title (N04–N07, E02–E05, U02–U05). The boards give each its own weight: a big orange 404 with a quiet centred card, a calm error card, an unboxed plan not found. Recognition suffers most on the 404 (N03, B-053).
  2. P2 · The run limit is buried. On a phone the alert starts 672 px down, under the act's buttons, the status line and the test-data banner (R05, R11); the one thing to read is the last thing seen.
  3. P2 · A promise with no path. “open one of your earlier plans” offers nothing to open (R09): an open question for DESIGN, but the copy shouldn't promise it until there is a link.
  4. P3 · Copy written for one failure. “while setting up the pitch” shows on every route error and on the global error, where nothing was being set up (E05, G03).
  5. P3 · The reference competes with the actions (E08): the board keeps it as a footnote under a rule (B-054).
