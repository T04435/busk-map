Process fixes

  • BRAND: every deck section gets a row in restyle-notes (“Landing /” listed 6 of 18 parts); the handoff is generated from the deck boards, not written separately.
  • DEV: self-check by section against the deck board at 3 widths (side-by-side screenshots), and list every unbuilt section as a deviation.
  • QA: build the inventory from the design before reading the build; specs assert each designed section exists, and focus checks compare focused vs unfocused styles.
  • MANAGER: track each brief item (e.g. the 8 judge additions) to a deck section and a build check, and close it only when all three exist.
  • All: a decision that changes a screen (v67, 121) is copied into the handoff and the checklist the same day, so DEV and QA verify against it.
