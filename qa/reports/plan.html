Impeccable critique (design review)

  1. P1 · The payoff is below the fold. “Your busk” starts at 871 px on a phone and 987 px at 1440: eyebrow, title, chips, two buttons, status line, funnel and Agent vs engine come first (B-050). The board puts title, one data line and the map up top.
  2. P1 · Dead ends. Qloo down (P17) and no legal pitch (P19) leave no next step; the board gives Try again, a general-permit plan or another day (B-043, B-044).
  3. P2 · Alert stacks. No match (P18) shows three alerts in a row; the user can't tell which matters.
  4. P2 · Jargon. “agent”, “engine”, “test model” in user copy where the board says Claude and Busk Map's scores (B-049).
  5. P2 · Weather is a sliver (P06): three cells give no sense of before/after the slot (B-047).
  6. P3 · Two map languages on one page (P05's live map vs the feed's hex map), and the map's tiles fight the cells.
