Impeccable critique (design review)

  1. P1 · The log is buried. The page leads with the form and an explainer card. The performer's own busks start well below the fold, and the board's two-column page (log left, form right) is lost at 1440 (T06, T24, T34, B-056).
  2. P1 · Errors that don't help. One error at a time under the button, with no field-level message or focus move (T33). If the log can't be read, the page says nothing is logged (T33d). People will think their takings are gone.
  3. P2 · The form breaks on a phone after you've planned. This is the common path: you plan, play, then log. The Busk select's long label pushes every field past the card edge at 390 (B-063).
  4. P2 · Examples drown the page. 27 example rows over three acts (T19, T22) make a 5,500 px page. One short example would show the idea.
  5. P3 · Words. “sessions” for busks, ISO dates and “12 pm–3 pm” (T13), and lines of 130+ characters (T05, T34).
